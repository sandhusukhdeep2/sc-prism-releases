WHAT THIS SOFTWARE DOES — PLEASE READ
-------------------------------------
SC Prism is an agent-orchestration tool. When you grant it the corresponding
permissions, it can:

  * execute shell commands on your computer, with your user account's full
    privileges;
  * read, create, modify, and delete files in folders you point it at;
  * capture your screen and control your keyboard and mouse;
  * send the contents of your messages, files, and tool results to whichever
    third-party AI providers you configure, over the internet.

These capabilities are OFF by default and require your explicit grant. You are
responsible for deciding what to grant, to which agents, and on what data.

SC Prism includes a keyword-based gate that pins text containing terms such as
"password", "credential", "api-key", "token", and "secret" to a local model so
it cannot be sent to a cloud provider. This is a word-level gate, not a content
classifier: it will not, on its own, recognise an unlabelled API key, an
account number, a home address, or other sensitive data that is not
accompanied by one of those words. Do not rely on it as your only safeguard.
For work that must never leave your machine, configure local-only routing.
